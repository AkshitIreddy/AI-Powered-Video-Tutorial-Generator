# Descriptive analysis for the synthetic class-score dataset

The ten rows are synthetic and do not describe real students.

Scores are `52,57,61,65,71,74,79,83,88,90`.

- Count: `10`
- Mean: `(52+57+61+65+71+74+79+83+88+90)/10 = 72`
- Median: `(71+74)/2 = 72.5`
- Minimum: `52`
- Maximum: `90`
- Range: `38`

The data show a positive association between study hours and score, but the synthetic table cannot establish that additional study caused a higher score. A chart must label both axes, preserve the individual observations, and avoid truncating the score axis in a way that exaggerates differences.
