# The fraction picnic

Mina and Ravi have two identical round flatbreads. They cut each flatbread into four equal pieces. One equal piece is one fourth. Two fourths cover the same amount as one half.

Mina places one fourth beside two fourths. The number on the bottom tells how many equal pieces make one whole. The number on top tells how many of those pieces we are counting.

The story uses food only as an illustration. It must show equal-sized pieces, avoid implying that the written number changes the amount of food, and use shape labels as well as color. The child is invited to predict whether `2/4` and `1/2` cover the same area before the pieces overlap.
