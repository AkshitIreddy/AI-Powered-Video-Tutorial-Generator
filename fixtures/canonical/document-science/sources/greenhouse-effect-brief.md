# How the greenhouse effect works

This locally authored test document supports a short document-grounded science tutorial.

Sunlight supplies energy to the Earth system. Some incoming sunlight is reflected, while absorbed energy warms the surface and atmosphere. The warm Earth emits infrared radiation. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared radiation at particular wavelengths, which changes the rate at which energy escapes to space. The natural greenhouse effect keeps Earth's surface warmer than it would otherwise be.

Increasing greenhouse-gas concentrations strengthens this effect and creates an energy imbalance until the climate system warms enough for outgoing energy to balance incoming energy again. The greenhouse effect is not primarily a literal solid “blanket,” and it is not caused by greenhouse gases trapping all heat permanently.

Clouds, water vapor, ice, oceans, land, and atmospheric circulation contribute feedbacks and redistribution. A short tutorial should distinguish the core radiative mechanism from these feedbacks rather than claim the system has only one component.
